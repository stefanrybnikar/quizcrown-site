QuizCrown app (Android, sk.mightyit.kviz) · effective 13 November 2026

Data stored on your phone

Your progress (XP, streak, completed lessons, questions to review, test results) and settings (language, content country, daily goal, appearance, reminders) are stored only on your phone. We never send them anywhere. If Android backup is on, the system may store them in a backup in your Google account – this is handled by Google under its privacy policy. You can delete the data in the profile (Reset all progress) or by uninstalling the app.

Error reports (Sentry)

When the app crashes or an error occurs, it sends an error report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), whose data is stored in the European Union. A report contains the error description, device model, Android version, app version, time and a random installation ID (it tells us how many devices an error affects; it is not linked to your identity and disappears when you uninstall). It contains no name, email, location or progress, and IP addresses are not stored. Reports are used only to fix bugs and are kept for at most 90 days. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Content updates (Expo)

On launch the app checks for new content and downloads it from the servers of Expo (650 Industries, Inc.). Only the technical data needed to deliver the update is sent (such as the app version and platform); like any internet server, it sees the device's IP address. Legal basis: legitimate interest in current and correct content.

Other features

  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the phone itself. Nothing is sent.
  • Sharing a daily quiz result and reporting a question open an app of your choice (for example email). We only learn about it if you send us a message. Emails are used only to reply and to fix content.
  • Rating prompts are shown by Google Play, which handles the rating.

Children

Children may use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, and there are no ads or in-app purchases.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase data, to restrict processing and to object. As we hold no data that identifies you, there is usually nothing to provide – but write to us and we will help. You can also contact the Slovak data protection authority, Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR.

Changes

If the way we process data changes (for example accounts or usage statistics are added), we will update this policy beforehand and mention the change in the app.
